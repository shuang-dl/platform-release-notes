Release v9.4.54 — 2026-07-29

View original Slack post · 1785329096.512189

Summary

This release ships 13 changes across PM Operations (creator/assignee avatars and chevron drill-downs in maintenance lists), Onboarding & Imports (tenant portal feature cards, an instant welcome email with set-password activation, and a redesigned Training Academy hub), Tenant Experience & Payments (AR dashboard daily breakdowns, tenant drawer fixes, a new-tab payment health drill, and a settings-dialog refresh), Workflows (Command Center execution details), Leads & Leasing (server-side holding-deposit withholding), and Banking (a modernized Usage & Limits list). Most items are incremental UI/UX polish; two items (the Training Academy redesign and the Risk Indicator settings dialog rebuild) are meta/internal changes rather than net-new customer features.
