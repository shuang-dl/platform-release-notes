Release v9.4.43 — 2026-07-26

View original Slack post · 1785068885.972669

Summary

This release refreshes the Incoming Payments Transactions tab in Accounting (BAN-1539) and ships a redesigned Choose Plan page plus more reliable server-side AI import mapping in Onboarding & Imports (ACT-1345, ACT-1104). Tenant Experience & Payments gets a Gmail-verification fix and a new bulk "request online payment" lease action (TEN-1090, TEN-1043), while Workflows adds a pre-activation agent test-run preview and a clearer Command Center Status column alongside three internal fixes (WOR-1421, WOR-1492, WOR-1487, WOR-1491). Leads & Leasing rounds out the release with an applicant-portal payment/fee-preview step, a listings-wizard revert, and a smarter eviction action recommendation (L2L-2149, L2L-2080, TEN-1004), plus an internal Banking reliability tool (BAN-1577).
