Summary

This release ships a single backend performance improvement in Tenant Experience & Payments: TEN-1153 adds Redis caching (with gzip compression) for AR dashboard lease and aging widget queries, collapsing redundant active-lease lookups and cutting the aging widget's load time from roughly 17.6s to ~1s on large accounts. The change is purely a server-side performance fix — dashboard behavior and data shown to users are unchanged, so no Help Center article or training video needs updating.
